        SESSION BOUNDARIES

        Privacy notes

        GhostChat limits its own persistence, but a session is not a promise that no copy can exist elsewhere.

        ⌁

        Session-scoped by design

        Room state, invitations, keys, and the visible message list live in memory while the room is active. GhostChat does not write them to files, localStorage, or sessionStorage.

        ▣

        Copies are still possible

        Other participants, clipboard managers, screenshots, the operating system, WebView caches, swap, and diagnostics may retain copies outside GhostChat’s control.

        ⌑

        Invite privacy

        Anyone who gets the invite code can request to join while the room is open. Share it through a channel you trust and clear copied values when finished.

        ×

        Ending clears this view

        Leaving or ending a session clears the room from this app view. That is best-effort cleanup, not forensic erasure or a guarantee of remote delivery.

        WHAT THE ROOM CAN PROVE

        Security model

        The interface only reports capabilities supplied by the GhostChat core. A successful button press is not a claim of remote receipt.

        ◎

        End-to-end encryption

        Messages unlock after GhostChat verifies the room invite and the owner confirms the join. Each room uses fresh session keys.

        ◌

        Private connections

        Room traffic uses GhostChat’s built-in private network connection. It does not fall back to ordinary internet connections or a public room directory.

        ↗

        Membership is visible

        Participants and presence are shown to room members. The room owner is also a member and can read messages; display names are not verified identities.

        ✓

        Delivery is unconfirmed

        A queued message means the core accepted it for transport. It does not confirm that peers received it. Check the room before retrying an unknown send.

        ABOUT THE PRODUCT

        GhostChat

        Invitation-only, session-scoped chat for at most three participants. No account, public feed, or room directory.
